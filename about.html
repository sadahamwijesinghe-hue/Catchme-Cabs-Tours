About Catchme Cabs & Tours

We are Sri Lanka’s premier private cab service and customized holiday chauffeur provider, delivering unforgettable island memories with safety, comfort, and authentic hospitality.

Our Journey

Setting the Benchmark for Reliable Travel Across Sri Lanka

Founded with a vision to eliminate the frustration of unpredictable taxi fares, delays, and poor communication, Catchme Cabs & Tours has grown into one of the most reliable and highly recommended private transport networks in Sri Lanka.

Whether welcoming foreign travelers stepping off long-haul flights at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA Katunayake) or chauffeuring families along the scenic hairpins of Nuwara Eliya and Ella, our promise remains steadfast: Punctual, spotless vehicles, honest fixed rates, and genuine Sri Lankan warmth.

Principles That Guide Us

The Catchme Cabs Promise

Every ride we operate is governed by these core standards of service excellence.

Safety Above All

Our drivers adhere strictly to speed limits and defensive driving techniques. All vehicles undergo comprehensive mechanical inspections and carry full passenger insurance.

Zero Hidden Surcharges

What you are quoted is what you pay. We do not surprise our passengers with unexpected night fees, waiting penalties, or fuel escalations. Honest hospitality is our trademark.

Vetted Professional Drivers

All our chauffeurs are police background-checked, courteous, English-fluent, and well-versed in Sri Lankan history, flora, fauna, and local dining gems.

Driver Excellence

More Than Just Drivers — Your Island Chauffeur Guides

A road trip in Sri Lanka is only as wonderful as the person behind the wheel. That’s why we select chauffeurs who share our dedication to hospitality, patience, and safety.

Fluent English Communication: Easy to converse, ask questions, and request customized stops along the journey.

Deep Local Route Knowledge: Safe navigation through mountain passes, tea country curves, and city traffic shortcuts.

Tourist Chauffeur Etiquette: Respectful, punctual, clean-uniformed, and attentive to family & child needs.
